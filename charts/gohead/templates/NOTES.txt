Create the first account (it becomes the admin):

  curl -X POST <api-url>/auth/register -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
    -d '{"username":"admin","email":"admin@example.com","password":"change-me-please"}'

Register it right away: until then anyone who can reach the API can claim it.
